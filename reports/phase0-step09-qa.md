# Phase 0 geographic and publication checks

Validated 1,222 site entries and 1,239 memberships against the reviewed
register, source coordinates and dated maturity evidence. All 10,788 observations
remain traceable. Every site ID has one maturity assignment and an explicit representative source.

Country checks used Natural Earth 1:50 million geometry with a 25 km border tolerance.
Outcomes: {'inside_country': 1190, 'outside_tolerance': 2, 'within_25_km': 30}. Unverified locations are held from the map and downloadable sites.
The separate private source archive retains the publisher's observations for inspection.
There are 227 groups with identical names and source points coinciding
to five decimal places. 482 entries have alternate publisher
views. These reviews preserve exact source identities and provisional labels.

The download policy checks every contributing source for confirmed reuse, known retrieval date,
source URL, grade and checksum. 282 sites qualify. Hold reasons overlap:
{'document_evidence': 2, 'document_retrieval': 2, 'document_terms': 4, 'member_country_unverified': 4, 'member_outside_tolerance': 2, 'outside_tolerance': 2, 'retrieval_date': 936, 'source_terms': 7}. Detailed dispositions are private and no source coordinate was moved.

The next stage validates the schemas of the export tables and tiles after serialization.
